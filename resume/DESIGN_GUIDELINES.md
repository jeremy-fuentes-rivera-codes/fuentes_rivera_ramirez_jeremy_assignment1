## 1. Design Principles
### Visuals
I chose the colors #000000, #FFFFFF, and #A09E9E because they look like a classic resume and provide high contrast, making it very good option for accesibility.

### Typograhy
The font family used throughout the code was selected readibility purposes. Also, it offers a clear, professional visual style similar to New Times Roman which I like.

### Layout and structure
I decided to structure my website like a traditional resume to convey a clean and professional layout.Below my name, I included links to my GitHub, LinkedIn, and certification to highlight my professional resources. Also, I organized my skills in a condensed paragraph format to save vertical space and prevent unnecessary scrolling. The remaining sections use bullet points since those parts typically follow that structure on a standard resume.
